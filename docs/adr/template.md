# NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Names

## Context

The problem and the forces at play. State facts, not the decision.

## Decision

The choice that was made, written in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work this creates.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was rejected, and why.
